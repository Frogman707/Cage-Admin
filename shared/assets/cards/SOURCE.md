# Card faces

The 52 rank faces are rasterized from **Vector Playing Cards** by Byron Knoll,
released by the author into the **public domain** — usable for any purpose with no
attribution required (the author asks only that it be appreciated, so: thank you).

- original SVG artwork: https://code.google.com/archive/p/vector-playing-cards/
- the SVG copies used here: https://github.com/hayeah/playing-cards-assets

Rendered to PNG at 224x325 through headless Chromium at the artwork's own viewBox
ratio (167.0869 x 242.667), then palette-quantized to 128 colours — 0.001% of pixels
differ from the full-colour render, and the deck weighs 366 KB instead of 1.9 MB.

`card_back.png` is not from that deck; it is drawn for this site in the AVATAR SPEED
navy/brass palette, on the same card silhouette so backs and faces line up.
